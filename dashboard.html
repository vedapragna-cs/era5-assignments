E1 — mixed vs pure lane shards (the session's open question)

armIndic by lane tag by document contentaudit
M_mixed16.39%44.26%NOT auditable
P_pure20.00%20.00%auditable

In arm P the scheduler's lane tag and the actual document content agree exactly, so the delivered Indic share is auditable straight from the consumption ledger. In arm M they diverge: the ledger reports the CARRIER lane, so the protected lane's true delivery is invisible to every downstream audit that reads mixture_lanes. That is the decisive difference - not the share itself but whether the share can be measured at all. A floor you cannot audit is not a floor, and S5's own measurement of the course's always-on lane (stated 8%, delivered ~15%) is exactly what an unauditable floor looks like.
